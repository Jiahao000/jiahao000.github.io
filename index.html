Jiahao Xie

I am currently a Ph.D. student at MMLab@NTU, Nanyang Technological University, supervised by Prof. Chen Change Loy and Prof. Yew Soon Ong. I also work closely with Prof. Ziwei Liu. Before that, I received my B.Eng. degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University.

My research interests include computer vision and deep learning, especially un-/self-supervised learning, representation learning. I am a core developer and maintainer of MMSelfSup, a popular OpenMMLab self-supervised learning toolbox and benchmark.
